# Exact-MPN replacement-parts resolver gate

## Pre-registered design

The first exact-MPN test is a resolver experiment, not an arbitrage backtest.
It asks whether identifiers native to replacement-parts listings create a deep
enough deterministic eBay market to justify source-price and margin work.

- Freeze 50 source products before any eBay lookup.
- Balance the basket across ten appliance and power-tool part categories.
- Search eBay US by source brand plus manufacturer part number.
- Confirm brand and MPN from each eBay item-detail record; do not use titles.
- Restrict coherent depth and prices to detail-confirmed `New` items.
- Reject conflicting pack or lot quantities.
- Pass only if at least 25% of the basket has three coherent active listings.

The basket was generated by stable SHA-256 ranking within each category from
the eReplacementParts part-detail sitemap dated 2026-08-15. The source sitemap
exposes category, OEM brand, a stable part record, and the manufacturer part
number in each product URL. The frozen basket SHA-256 is
`d5934bb2a51532ddead6d761dd9f31eb6ecce3d000344a8dac54787037791812`.

## Result

The live run completed on 2026-08-24 against `EBAY_US`. Search was limited to
the first 20 new, fixed-price results per identity, and every returned result
was inspected through eBay item detail. The keyset's bulk-detail method was not
authorized, so the client used its tested individual-detail fallback.

| Measure | Result |
| --- | ---: |
| Queries with active search results | 43 / 50 (86%) |
| Identities with exact brand + MPN confirmation | 32 / 50 (64%) |
| Identities with at least 3 coherent new listings | 16 / 50 (32%) |
| Median coherent depth across the full basket | 1 |
| Pre-registered pass threshold | 13 / 50 (25% minimum) |

Statuses were 16 `EXACT_USABLE`, 14 `EXACT_SHALLOW`, 11 `UNCONFIRMED`, seven
`NOT_FOUND`, and two `AMBIGUOUS`. The ambiguous identities had conflicting
pack or lot quantities and contributed zero coherent depth.

Appliance parts produced 10 usable markets from 25 identities; tool parts
produced six from 25. Dryers were strongest at 4/5 usable, followed by
refrigerators at 3/5. No circular-saw or upright-vacuum identity passed in this
small basket, so those category rates are directional rather than estimates.

**Decision: the exact-MPN resolver gate PASSED at 32%.** Exact-MPN replacement
parts remain open and advance to a separate acquisition-and-margin gate. This
does not establish profitable spreads: active asks are not sold prices, median
depth remains one across the full basket, and usable identities show meaningful
price dispersion. Source prices, stock, shipping, fees, condition-adjusted sold
evidence, and turnover must be measured before any item becomes actionable.
