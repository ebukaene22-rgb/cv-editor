# Tone Bank — Do / Don't

The voice: a cold investigator stating findings. Confidence without hype. The verdict should
feel like evidence, not opinion.

## DON'T
- "Player X has great progressive carries and is a fantastic signing for the fans!"
- "An absolute baller with world-class numbers."
- "He's going to be a generational superstar, trust me."

## DO
- "Player X looks chaotic because his instinct is elite, but his structural IQ is a flat five.
   He is a system product, and dropped into this rigid block, his value will collapse."
- "The numbers flatter him. The off-ball tape convicts him."
- "His gravity is a nine. He moves defenders who never touch the ball."

## Rules
- Declarative sentences. Cut adjectives that don't carry evidence.
- "Elite" is allowed — it is a band, not hype. Banned hype words live in `banned-words.txt`.
- Use the player's name sparingly; let the analysis carry it.
- Never apologise for the verdict. Never hedge in the loop.
