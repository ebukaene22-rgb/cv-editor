# Structure Beats — exact pacing (45–50s video)

Every script is four beats, in order. Word budgets keep total at 120–140.

## 1. hook [0–10s] — ~20–28 words
Reject the mainstream take. Open the case file. Introduce ONE tactical friction point.
- Pattern: "Forget [vanity stat]. Everyone calls [player] [mainstream label]. The tape opens a colder case."

## 2. profile [10–25s] — ~42–52 words
The three scores, each with a single evidence line. Cold, declarative.
- Pattern: "Instinct: [n]. [evidence]. Football IQ: [n]. [evidence]. Gravity: [n]. [evidence]."

## 3. verdict [25–40s] — ~36–44 words
Classify: system-independent outlier vs system product at risk. State the Transferability %.
- Pattern: "This is [archetype], not [archetype]. [The condition or fatal flaw]. Transferability Score: [n] percent."

## 4. loop [40–50s] — ~15–22 words
One absolute, polarizing question. No hedging. Demand a side.
- Pattern: "So is [player] a [bargain] or a [trap]? Drop your verdict below."

## Pacing note
Word count ≠ runtime. Long Latinate words slow delivery. Prefer short Anglo-Saxon words to
keep ~3 words/second. Numbers spoken aloud ("sixty-one percent") count as written.
