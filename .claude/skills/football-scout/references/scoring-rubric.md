# Scoring Rubric — anchoring Instinct / IQ / Gravity (1–10)

Scores are **editorial but anchored**. Every score must point to specific evidence in the
dossier (a role, a phase of play, a recurring pattern). Never round to "8 because he's good."

## General band meaning

| Band | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| 9–10 | Outlier. Defines this trait at the top of the sport. |
| 7–8  | Elite, but with a named limitation. |
| 5–6  | Functional. Survives, doesn't dictate. |
| 3–4  | Liability in this dimension; system has to hide it. |
| 1–2  | Actively costs the team. |

## Axis anchors

### Instinct (subconscious / transition / chaos)
- **9–10:** first action fires before the defender registers danger; thrives when structure breaks.
- **7–8:** sharp in transition but needs a half-second of space to trigger.
- **5–6:** reads obvious cues, freezes in genuine chaos.
- **≤4:** hesitates, defaults to the safe backward option under pressure.

### Football IQ (conscious / spacing / tempo)
- **9–10:** orchestrates tempo, manipulates opponents into the shape he wants.
- **7–8:** strong positional sense, occasional tempo misread in a low block.
- **5–6:** occupies the right zone but doesn't bend the game; needs instructions.
- **≤4:** drifts, breaks the team's structure, kills overloads.

### Gravity (off-ball distortion)
- **9–10:** two defenders track him with no ball; backline bends, weak side opens.
- **7–8:** pulls a marker reliably, distorts one channel.
- **5–6:** noticed only when on the ball.
- **≤4:** ignored off the ball; defenders rest against him.

## Anti-patterns
- Do not give matching scores across axes "to be safe" — the contrast is the content.
- A high Instinct + low IQ profile is the channel's signature: chaotic-looking, system-fragile.
